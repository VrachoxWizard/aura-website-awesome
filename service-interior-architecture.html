The Studio / Services / Interior Architecture

Interior Architecture

Intimate micro-environments shaped through bespoke timber joinery, hand-quarried natural stone, integrated lighting, and sensory acoustic calm.

Tactile Transitions & Domestic Stillness

Where external architecture defines boundaries and shelter, interior architecture shapes the human sensory experience. At Auren Studio, we approach interior spaces as atmospheric chambers where light, raw textures, and silent acoustic proportions converge into quiet everyday luxury.

We curate spaces that feel naturally composed rather than staged. By studying the daily rituals of our clients—from morning sun angles over a kitchen counter to the evening shelter of a library—we choreograph continuous, uninterrupted spatial transitions.

Bespoke Joinery & Quarry Curation

Our studio collaborates with master carpenters, stonemasons, and bronze casters across Switzerland, Italy, and Scandinavia. We design integrated millwork that conceals mechanical infrastructure while celebrating the raw character of untreated white oak, brushed travertine, and honed basalt.

Led by partner Sofia Bennett, our interior team develops customized furniture pieces, acoustic timber wall paneling, and atmospheric lighting scenes calibrated specifically for each home or boutique hospitality retreat.

Capabilities & Scope

01. Spatial Flow & Circulation

Micro-environment zoning, ergonomic circulation pathways, sightline framing, and acoustic zoning.

02. Custom Millwork & Joinery

Architectural cabinetry, concealed storage systems, pivot doors, and bespoke architectural metalwork.

03. Quarry Stone & Timber Library

Direct sourcing of rare marble, travertine, quartzite, and responsibly harvested European hard timbers.

04. Circadian Light & Atmosphere

Indirect ambient illumination, architectural coves, shadow manipulation, and automated warm-dim programming.

05. Furniture & Object Curation

Custom furniture prototyping, vintage design collectible acquisition, and tactile textile specification.
