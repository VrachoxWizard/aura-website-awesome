Raw Materiality: Why Untreated Stone and White Oak Outlast Trends

Sofia Bennett · October 28, 2026 · 5 min read · Materiality & Craft

Materials possess their own temporal memory. When surfaces are stripped of glossy artificial lacquers and allowed to remain honest, they do not degrade—they patina, absorbing life, sunlight, and touch with graceful resilience.

In a design landscape increasingly saturated with composite polymers and printed ceramic imitations, authentic tactile substance has become rare. Our studio maintains that a space should reveal the true origins of its geology and forestry.

The Dignity of the Patina

Synthetic materials attempt to freeze time. They look pristine on the day of installation, yet the moment a scratch or dent appears, they are permanently broken. Natural materials, by contrast, are living records of human existence.

A slab of open-pore travertine gently softens with touch; untreated European white oak silvers under the caress of southern daylight; solid bronze oil-rubs where fingers grasp a door handle, revealing warm gold beneath dark oxidation. This living quality endows a home with soul.

“Authentic design requires courage: the courage to embrace imperfections, mineral veins, and the slow, beautiful surrender of stone to time.”

— Sofia Bennett, Partner & Material Specialist

Sourcing with Restraint

Our studio regularly visits family-run alpine quarries in Switzerland and central Italy. We select individual blocks rather than pre-cut catalog slabs, studying how natural quarry bedding planes can be aligned to create continuous sculptural transitions across bathrooms, fireplaces, and kitchen islands.

By partnering with generational artisans who understand the grain of timber and the cleavage of stone, we ensure that every millwork component and threshold is built to endure for decades.

Sofia Bennett

Partner at Auren Studio leading interior architecture, tactile material curation, quarry sourcing, and custom furniture prototyping across international residential projects.
