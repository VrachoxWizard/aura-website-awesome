Architectural Design

Sculpting residential sanctuaries and cultural landmarks responsive to site topography, solar geometry, and enduring structural purity.

Form, Proportion & Topography

At Auren Studio, architecture begins with listening to the natural terrain. We do not impose generic forms upon a landscape; rather, we carve volumes that channel prevailing winds, frame celestial daylight, and establish quiet visual dialogue with surrounding stone, forest, or coastline.

Our architectural practice strips away superfluous decorative layers to arrive at pure spatial balance. We orchestrate transitions between expansive communal rooms and deeply sheltered private nooks, ensuring that modern living feels rooted, calm, and emotionally grounding.

Material Permanence & Thermal Craft

We construct with honest monolithic materials—board-formed architectural concrete, thermal limestone, rammed earth, and mass timber. By integrating passive solar orientation and natural ventilation corridors, our buildings maintain acoustic stillness and environmental resilience across generations.

Each commission is spearheaded directly by founding partners Marcus Vance and Elena Rostova, who lead dedicated project teams from initial physical modeling through on-site execution.

Capabilities & Scope

01. Site & Climate Feasibility

Topographical mapping, sun-path analysis, zoning envelope assessment, and historical context synthesis.

02. Schematic Concept & Modeling

Spatial flow sequencing, physical plaster and timber scale models, and 3D daylight rendering.

03. Engineering Coordination

Seamless structural integration with acoustic, geotechnical, and MEP specialist consultants.

04. Technical Documentation

Exhaustive millimeter-tolerance construction drawings, schedules, and custom material specifications.

05. On-Site Executive Stewardship

Direct architectural oversight throughout all build phases to ensure uncompromised quality.
