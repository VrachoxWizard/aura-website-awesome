The Studio / Privacy & Compliance

Privacy Policy

Our commitment to client confidentiality, data stewardship, and dignified privacy practices in accordance with Swiss FADP and European GDPR regulations.

1. Studio Data Philosophy

At Auren Studio (Auren LLC, headquartered in Zürich, Switzerland), privacy is fundamental to our practice. We design private residences and bespoke spatial sanctuaries for individuals and organizations who value discretion. We handle your personal and architectural data with the same rigor, restraint, and discretion that defines our spatial design.

This Privacy Notice outlines how we collect, store, and safeguard information gathered through our digital atelier (aurenstudio.com) and our physical studios in Zürich and Berlin.

2. Data We Collect

We only collect information necessary to fulfill architectural inquiries, evaluate client commissions, or communicate studio publications:

  • Inquiry Details: Name, professional email address, telephone number, geographic project coordinates, and project scope submitted via our commission inquiry forms.
  • Technical Metadata: Anonymized server logs, browser type, device resolution, and approximate geographic region collected to deliver responsive performance and 60fps animations.
  • Studio Dispatch Subscribers: Email address provided voluntarily for receiving our quarterly architectural journal.

3. How We Use Client Information

We process your data strictly under lawful bases (Article 6 GDPR and Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection):

  • To review and respond to architectural commission requests and client consultations.
  • To prepare project proposals, feasibility evaluations, and contract documentation.
  • To maintain studio communications and dispatch our architectural publications when requested.
  • We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade client personal information to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Storage & Security Safeguards

All electronic client project files, architectural schematics, and communication records are encrypted at rest and in transit using modern cryptographic protocols (AES-256). Data centers utilized by Auren Studio are located within Switzerland and the European Union, benefiting from rigorous Swiss data protection standards.

Access to client identities and residential project coordinates is strictly restricted to senior partners and project team members on a need-to-know basis.

5. Your Rights & Contact

Under applicable Swiss and European data protection laws, you retain the right to:

  • Request access to copies of your personal data held by Auren Studio.
  • Request rectification of inaccurate or outdated information.
  • Request complete deletion ("right to be forgotten") of your inquiry records.
  • Revoke consent for our newsletter dispatch at any moment with a single click.

To exercise any of these rights or speak directly with our studio data compliance officer, contact us at privacy@aurenstudio.com or via mail:
