The Studio / Careers & Culture

Careers

We are an intimate atelier of architects, interior designers, and visual artists dedicated to craft, spatial tranquility, and precision.

LIFE AT AUREN

An environment designed for deep focus and craft

We eschew rapid production cycles in favor of intentional architectural depth. Our team members work directly alongside the four founding partners on every project, maintaining close contact with craftspeople, builders, and clients.

Whether in our daylight-flooded Zürich atelier or Berlin satellite workspace, we prioritize intellectual curiosity, generous time for design exploration, and healthy work-life balance.

Rigorous Craftsmanship

We build full-scale physical mockups, study stone slabs at quarries, and test lighting temperatures before committing to construction drawings.

Collaborative Autonomy

Project architects lead initiatives from concept through ribbon-cutting, backed by direct mentorship from studio founders.

Holistic Human Scale

We respect individual rhythm, encourage continued research and sabbatical travel, and cultivate an ego-free collaborative studio ethos.
